Common questions

What to know before you start.

The current release is an open beta. Use it on real projects, but retain your normal technical checks and project controls.

Is EV Site Planner really free?

Yes. The current beta is free to use and does not require an account or payment card. Paid features may be introduced later, but the page will be updated before that changes.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for EV installers, electrical contractors, surveyors, estimators, project managers and client teams planning domestic or commercial chargepoint installations.

Does it replace electrical design or certification?

No. It supports planning, visual communication and project documentation. The final design, regulatory assessment, installation and certification remain the responsibility of the relevant competent persons.

Can I use it for both homes and commercial sites?

Yes. The planner includes distinct domestic and commercial workflows so the information, symbols and outputs can match the scale of the proposed installation.

How can I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Use the feedback link in the footer to report a snag or suggest a feature. Clear examples, screenshots and the steps that caused an issue are especially helpful during beta.
